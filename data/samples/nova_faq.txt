Nova Robotics - Frequently Asked Questions

Who founded Nova Robotics?
Nova Robotics was founded in 2019 in the city of Port Elan by Lina Haddad (CEO) and Omar Saleh (CTO).

What does Nova Robotics make?
We make warehouse robots. Our main product is Kito, a robot that moves shelves inside warehouses.
A smaller model, Kito Mini, is designed for small shops and carries up to 40 kg.

When is customer support available?
Support is available Sunday to Thursday, from 9:00 to 17:00 (Port Elan time).
You can reach us at support@novarobotics.example.

How long is the warranty?
Every Kito robot comes with a 2-year warranty. Batteries are covered for 1 year only.

Do you offer training?
Yes. Every new customer gets a free 3-hour online training session for up to 5 employees.
